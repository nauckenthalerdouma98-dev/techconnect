Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using TechConnect — from signing up to getting paid. If you don’t find your answer, reach out via our support panel.

TechConnect is a secure digital marketplace that connects skilled freelancers with clients in Cameroon and beyond. Payments are held in Nkwa Pay escrow and only released after milestone approval, giving both sides total confidence.

Click “Join free” on the landing page. You’ll choose whether you’re a Skilled Individual or a Client, then register with your email and password. After that you can set up your profile, list skills, or post a job.

Absolutely. All payments are handled through Nkwa Pay’s escrow system. The client deposits funds before work begins, and the money is only released when the client approves the completed milestone. TechConnect never touches the funds directly.

When a client posts a job, they break it into stages (milestones). For each milestone, the client deposits the corresponding amount. The freelancer submits the work; after client approval, the funds (minus our 10% commission) are sent to the freelancer. If changes are needed, the freelancer can revise up to three times per milestone.

TechConnect deducts a 10% commission from every payment released to a freelancer. For example, on a 20,000 FCFA milestone, the freelancer receives 18,000 FCFA. There are no hidden fees — you always see the net amount before you accept the job.

Anyone with a valid email address and internet connection. Skilled individuals can be coders, designers, writers, video editors, virtual assistants — any digital skill is welcome. Clients are typically small businesses, startups, NGOs, or individuals with a project.

After a job is completed, both the client and the freelancer can rate each other on a 1‑5 star scale and leave an optional comment. Those ratings build your public reputation. Freelancers with an average of 4.5+ (after 10 jobs) earn a “Top Rated” badge.

If the client and freelancer can’t agree on a milestone, the escrowed funds are frozen. Our admin team steps in, reviews the work and communication, and mediates a fair resolution. Only the admin can release funds after a dispute.

For the initial launch, the platform operates in Central African CFA francs (FCFA) and focuses on the Cameroonian market. We plan to expand to other countries and currencies in the future, including mobile money options.
