Nkwa Pay escrow

Your money, protected every step

TechConnect never lets funds flow directly between parties. Nkwa Pay holds every payment in escrow until the client confirms the work is done.

  • 1

    Client deposits funds

    Before work begins, the client deposits the milestone amount into our Nkwa Pay escrow account. The job only activates after confirmation.

  • 2

    Work & milestone approval

    The freelancer submits deliverables. The client reviews and approves — or requests changes (max 3 revision rounds per milestone).

  • 3

    Funds released on approval

    On approval, the freelancer receives the amount minus the 10% platform commission. Disputes are frozen and escalated to our admin team.
